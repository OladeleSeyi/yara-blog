Revolut makes it simple to hold and send cryptocurrency directly from your account. When you send crypto through Revolut to Yara, you're choosing one of the fastest and most cost-effective ways to get money to Nigeria. This guide will walk you through the entire process, from selecting your token to completing your transfer in just a few minutes.

- **Secure**: Both Revolut and Yara use industry-leading security protocols

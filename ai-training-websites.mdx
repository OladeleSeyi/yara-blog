The research and development departments of AI companies need people to review AI-generated answers, evaluate search results, label data, write prompts, transcribe audio, and use their professional knowledge to make AI models better. You do not necessarily need to be a software engineer to get started in the AI training space.

For Ghanaians and Nigerians looking to earn online, several platforms offer AI training and data-related opportunities. However, availability and eligibility can vary by project.

Here are seven platforms worth checking in 2026.

## 1. Mindrift

[Mindrift](https://mindrift.ai/) connects professionals and subject-matter experts with projects designed to improve generative AI models. Tasks can include evaluating AI responses, creating prompts, writing, fact-checking, and reviewing information. The platform works with people across fields including STEM, law, writing, and linguistics.

Mindrift currently advertises project-based AI training opportunities, including specialist roles and entry-level tasks. Compensation varies by project and expertise.

- **Best for:** Writers, researchers, professionals, and subject-matter experts.
- **Potential work:** AI evaluation, prompt creation, writing, and fact-checking.
- **Pay:** Project-specific; advertised rates can vary significantly by expertise and task.

## 2. CrowdGen by Appen

[CrowdGen](https://crowdgen.com/) is Appen's contributor platform for AI training and data projects. Depending on the project, you could evaluate AI-generated content, annotate data, generate examples, improve model responses, or complete language-related tasks.

CrowdGen has had projects advertised for people in Nigeria and Ghana, although availability changes frequently.

- **Best for:** Beginners interested in data annotation, AI evaluation, and language work.
- **Pay:** Varies by project, task, and location.

## 3. OneForma

[OneForma](https://www.oneforma.com/) offers remote projects across areas such as search relevance, data collection, transcription, annotation, OCR, document processing, and AI-response evaluation.

Each project has its own requirements, so you should check the country, language, device, and skill requirements before applying.

- **Best for:** Beginners and experienced freelancers interested in AI, language, and data projects.
- **Potential work:** Search evaluation, transcription, annotation, and data collection.
- **Pay:** Project-specific.

## 4. TELUS Digital AI Community

[TELUS Digital's AI Community](https://jobs.telusdigital.com/jobs/search) brings together contributors who help improve search engines, AI systems, and online content. Work can include evaluating search results, reviewing online content, annotating data, and assessing AI-generated information.

Location eligibility is important. TELUS projects can be highly location-specific, so check the individual listing before applying.

- **Best for:** People interested in search evaluation, data annotation, and AI quality assurance.
- **Pay:** Varies by project and country.

## 5. Micro1

[Micro1](https://www.micro1.ai/) connects professionals with remote opportunities, including AI training and data-annotation projects. Depending on your background, you may evaluate AI outputs, write prompts, review responses, create training data, or work on technical AI projects.

Micro1 also has an option for experts interested in flexible AI training roles across different domains.

- **Best for:** Developers, product managers, writers, and other technical professionals.
- **Potential work:** AI training, evaluation, annotation, and technical AI projects.
- **Pay:** Project-specific, with specialist roles potentially paying more than basic annotation work.

## 6. OpenTrain AI

If you are specifically looking for AI training opportunities in Nigeria, [OpenTrain AI](https://www.opentrain.ai/jobs/country/nigeria/) is worth checking. Its Nigeria job page lists remote AI training and data-labeling roles and allows applicants to filter opportunities by location and category.

Opportunities can include AI-response evaluation, data annotation, coding, audio and speech work, data analysis, image and video annotation, and specialist roles.

- **Best for:** Professionals, developers, analysts, researchers, and specialists.
- **Potential work:** AI evaluation, data labeling, coding, transcription, and specialist AI training.
- **Pay:** Varies significantly by project.

## 7. LXT

[LXT](https://www.lxt.ai/jobs/) provides data used to train and improve AI systems and works with remote contributors on data-collection and annotation projects.

Tasks can involve images, video, audio, speech, text, and other forms of data annotation. Individual projects may still have specific language, demographic, device, or location requirements.

- **Best for:** People interested in data collection, annotation, transcription, and language projects.
- **Pay:** Depends on the project and task.

## What Do You Actually Do in an AI Training Job?

AI training is often simpler than it sounds. You might be asked to:

- Compare two AI answers and choose the better one
- Check whether an AI response is accurate
- Write questions that test an AI model
- Label images or videos
- Review search results
- Transcribe or record audio

## How Much Can You Earn?

There is no standard salary for AI training work. Some platforms pay per task, while others advertise hourly rates. Your earnings can depend on your expertise, location, the project, task difficulty, qualification tests, and the amount of work available.

Some specialist projects advertise rates of tens of dollars per hour or more, while basic annotation and data-collection work can pay considerably less.

Do not assume the highest advertised rate is what you will earn. Always check the actual compensation for the project you qualify for.

## How to Increase Your Chances

1. **Highlight your expertise.** Do not simply describe yourself as a freelancer. If you are a doctor, developer, lawyer, teacher, or writer, make that expertise clear.
2. **Apply to multiple platforms.** AI training work is often project-based. One platform may have plenty of work this month and very little the next.
3. **Take assessments seriously.** Many platforms use qualification tests to determine which projects you can access.
4. **Check Nigeria or Ghana eligibility.** A platform can operate globally while a specific project is not available in Nigeria. Always check the individual listing.
5. **Watch out for scams.** Be careful with anyone asking you to pay money or provide login credentials to "unlock" an AI training job.

Platform availability, project eligibility, compensation, and payment methods can change. Always verify the requirements of the specific project before applying.
